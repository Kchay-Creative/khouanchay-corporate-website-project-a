# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Khouanchay Trading managers and administrators use the Management area across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Public visitors use the surrounding corporate website. Management users need to inspect an organization hierarchy quickly, while administrators also maintain the development-only structure.

## Product Purpose

Provide a stable corporate website shell around a permission-protected internal Management application. The current Management workflow presents and edits a demonstration organization hierarchy while preserving clear role boundaries and a structure that can grow into future internal modules.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, and approachable. The interface should feel established and slightly premium, with controlled use of Khouanchay yellow and blue and practical information density.

## Anti-references

Avoid SaaS landing-page patterns, experimental portfolio styling, generic admin templates, excessive animation, glass effects, heavy gradients, decorative dashboards, and invented business metrics. Management should not feel like a separate product from the corporate website.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user focused on the current Management task.
- Make role boundaries and editing state unmistakable.
- Use familiar controls and reveal supporting information when requested.
- Preserve one coherent Khouanchay identity across public and protected routes.
- Design every layout and text container for Lao, Thai, and English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced motion, usable touch targets, and responsive layouts. Lao is the default language, with complete Thai and English support. Text must remain readable when translated and must not rely on color alone to communicate state.
